## Campaign truth note (withdrawal of the earlier 100/100 claim)

The earlier version of this scorecard claimed **100/100** across all ten dimensions
with a weighted total of **100.0/100**. That claim was **invalid and is
withdrawn**. It overstated the state of the evidence: the NASA TMR flat-plate
benchmark failed, the V&V channel diagnostic was not a pass, and the certification
dossiers were NOT YET CERTIFIED (and remain so). Certification and a 100/100
score are **not claimed while benchmarks fail**. This revision records the
measured state as of 2026-11-01, with failures kept visible and fail-closed.
